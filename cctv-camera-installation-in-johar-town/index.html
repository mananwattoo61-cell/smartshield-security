Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in my area?

Prices depend on camera count, camera type, recorder and installation requirements. In Lahore, professional installation for basic setups typically starts from PKR 12,500, and most home and small-shop jobs fall between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000+. Your final quotation is confirmed after a site assessment.

Can I view my cameras on my phone?

Yes, where your system supports remote viewing, we configure mobile access and show you how to use live viewing and playback.

How long does installation take?

Most home and small-shop installations are completed in a day; larger systems take longer. The timeline is confirmed in your proposal.
