IP Camera Prices in Lahore

IP cameras send video over your network, which gives higher-resolution images, flexible placement and remote viewing. They cost more than analogue cameras, but they are the right choice for properties that need sharp video, Power over Ethernet cabling or a system that can grow camera by camera.

What Affects the Price?

  • Resolution — 2MP, 4MP, 5MP or 4K
  • Brand and build quality (Hikvision, Dahua, IMOU, CP Plus)
  • PoE switch and network cabling
  • NVR channel count and hard-disk size
  • AI features — only where camera and NVR both support them

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are IP cameras better than analogue cameras?

IP cameras give higher resolution and more flexible placement, and they are easier to expand. Analogue systems can be cheaper for simple setups. The right choice depends on your property and budget.

Do IP cameras need internet to record?

No. Recording to the NVR happens on your local network. Internet is only needed for remote viewing on your phone.

What is PoE in an IP camera system?

Power over Ethernet sends power and video through one network cable, which simplifies cabling. A PoE switch or PoE NVR is required.
