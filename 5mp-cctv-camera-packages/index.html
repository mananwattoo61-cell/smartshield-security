5MP CCTV Camera Packages in Lahore

A 5MP camera package sits between 4MP and 4K: noticeably sharper than 2MP, with more detail for entrances, parking areas and shop floors, while needing less storage and bandwidth than a full 4K system. A typical package includes the cameras, a compatible DVR, a hard disk, power supply and cabling, plus installation.

About prices on this page: package prices are listed exactly as published. Your final quotation is confirmed after a site assessment, because camera count, cable length, recorder and installation requirements differ for every property. What Affects the Price?

  • Number of cameras in the package (2, 4, 8 or more)
  • Camera type — dome, bullet or a mix
  • DVR channel count and hard-disk size
  • Cable length and installation complexity
  • Mobile viewing and remote access setup

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 5MP better than 4MP?

Yes, slightly — 5MP records more detail than 4MP, which helps for faces and number plates at moderate distances. The difference is smaller than the jump from 2MP to 4MP.

What is included in a 5MP CCTV package?

Usually the cameras, a compatible DVR, a hard disk sized to your retention period, power supply, cabling and installation, plus mobile viewing setup. The exact list is in your package proposal.

Does 5MP need more storage?

Yes — higher resolution uses more hard-disk space for the same retention period. Storage is sized around your camera count and how many days you want to keep recordings.
