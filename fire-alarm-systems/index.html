Fire Alarm Systems in Lahore

A fire alarm system gives early warning when smoke or heat is detected — the extra minutes it provides can protect lives and property. SmartShield Security supplies and installs fire alarm systems for homes, offices, shops, schools and commercial buildings in Lahore.

What a Fire Alarm System Includes

  • Smoke detectors — for rooms, corridors and common areas
  • Heat detectors — for kitchens and areas where smoke detectors give false alarms
  • Alarm panel — shows which zone triggered the alarm
  • Sirens and sounders — audible warning across the building
  • Manual call points — so anyone can raise the alarm

Where Fire Alarms Matter Most

Kitchens, electrical rooms, server rooms, storage areas, corridors and stairwells — and any area where people sleep or work. Placement depends on the building layout and how it is used.

Fire Alarms and CCTV Together

Fire alarms warn people; CCTV records what happened. Together they cover both early warning and incident review. Many commercial clients install both systems at the same time.

Testing and Maintenance

Detectors and panels should be tested regularly — a system that has not been tested may not work when it is needed. We provide testing and maintenance visits for installed fire alarm systems.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a fire alarm system for my shop or office?

It depends on the building size, use and local requirements. For larger premises and places where people gather, early warning is strongly recommended. We can assess your building and advise what is suitable.

How much does a fire alarm system cost?

It depends on the number of detectors, the size of the building and the panel type. Contact us with your building details for a quotation after assessment.

Do smoke detectors give false alarms?

They can, especially in kitchens or dusty areas. Heat detectors are used in those places instead, and detector placement is planned to reduce false alarms.
