Common Problems We Fix

Why CCTV Systems Fail — and How We Avoid It

Most CCTV problems are not caused by the camera itself, but by poor installation, wrong equipment or missing configuration. These are the faults we see most often in Lahore:

Poor Video Quality

Blurry or unclear footage usually comes from the wrong camera for the distance or lighting, dirty lenses, or low-quality cabling. We match resolution and lens to the area being covered.

Unstable Connections

Cameras that keep disconnecting are often caused by weak Wi-Fi, overloaded networks or poor power supply. We check the network and power before recommending wireless cameras.

Bad Camera Angles

A camera pointed at the wrong place records everything except the incident. Placement is planned around entrances, blind spots and the areas that actually matter.

Power Outages & Load Shedding

When power drops, recording stops unless backup is planned. We discuss UPS and backup options so your system keeps recording during outages where possible.

Mobile App Not Connecting

Remote viewing fails when recorder settings, network ports or app configuration are wrong. We configure and test mobile viewing on your own phone before handover.

Recording Failures

Systems that do not record are usually let down by a full or faulty hard disk, wrong retention settings or a recorder fault. We size storage to your retention period and test recording before sign-off.

Installation Process

How to Set Up a CCTV Camera — Step by Step

This is the process we follow on every SmartShield installation:

1. Choose Camera Locations

Cover entry points first — main gate, doors, driveway and cash counter — then boundaries, corridors and stock areas.

2. Mount the Cameras

Cameras are fixed securely with proper brackets at the right height and angle, sheltered from direct rain where possible.

3. Connect to DVR/NVR or Wi-Fi

Wired cameras connect to the recorder by cable; wireless cameras join your Wi-Fi network. Power over Ethernet is used for IP cameras where suitable.

4. Power the System

Power supplies are connected and checked. Backup power options are discussed for areas affected by outages.

5. Test the Video Feed

Every camera is checked live for picture quality, angle and night vision before the job is signed off.

6. Set Up Mobile Viewing

The viewing app is configured on your phone, live view and playback are tested, and we show you how to use them.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost of installing a CCTV camera?

The cost depends on the camera type (wired or wireless), its resolution and how complex the installation is. In Lahore, professional installation for basic setups typically starts from PKR 12,500. The final price is confirmed after a site assessment, because cable length, camera positions and recorder requirements differ for every property.

How much does CCTV cost to install?

CCTV installation prices in Lahore usually range between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000 or more, depending on the number of cameras, the DVR or NVR system and mobile connectivity setup. Customised packages are available for homes, offices and shops — use the Smart Calculator for a preliminary estimate.

How to set up a CCTV camera step by step?

Choose camera locations covering entry points, mount the cameras with screws and brackets, connect them to the DVR/NVR or Wi-Fi, attach cables or configure the wireless connection, power the system and test the video feed, then set up mobile viewing through the app. For a hassle-free installation, professional fitting avoids the wiring and configuration mistakes that cause most faults.

How much to install CCTV at home?

Home CCTV installation in Lahore typically starts at PKR 12,500 for a basic wireless setup. Costs increase with advanced features such as motion detection, night vision and mobile connectivity, and with the number of cameras and the size of the house.

Can I install CCTV cameras myself?

Yes, you can install a CCTV camera yourself if you have the right tools and technical knowledge. However, professional installation ensures correct wiring, secure setup and mobile access without errors — and it is usually the safer choice for multi-camera systems.

Can home CCTV work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Wired CCTV systems record directly to DVR or NVR devices without any internet connection. Wi-Fi or internet is only required if you want remote viewing on your mobile phone.

Which home CCTV camera is best?

For home security in Lahore, IMOU and Hikvision are among the most reliable choices, offering good performance, HD resolution and mobile connectivity. The best camera for your home still depends on indoor or outdoor use, distance and lighting.

What is the price of a 4MP CCTV camera in Pakistan?

A 4MP CCTV camera in Pakistan typically costs between PKR 6,000 and PKR 12,000 depending on the brand and features. Installation charges are separate, and the final system price depends on the recorder, storage and cabling as well.

Is a Wi-Fi CCTV camera good?

Yes. Wi-Fi CCTV cameras are convenient for homes and small offices. They offer easy setup, no heavy wiring and direct mobile app connectivity for remote monitoring — provided your Wi-Fi signal is strong where the cameras are placed.

Which CCTV camera brand is best in Pakistan?

Some of the best CCTV camera brands in Pakistan include Hikvision, Dahua, IMOU and CP Plus. We recommend a brand based on your budget and requirements, for long-term reliability and clear footage.
