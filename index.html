FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in Lahore?

Cost depends on camera count, camera type, recorder, storage and cabling. Use the Smart Calculator for a preliminary estimate, then contact us for a final quotation after a site assessment.

How do I choose the right camera?

It depends on indoor or outdoor use, distance, lighting and whether you need features such as night vision or AI detection. Tell us about your property and we will recommend a suitable setup.

Do AI cameras really detect people and vehicles?

Only when the camera and the recorder both support the feature. We check compatibility before recommending AI detection, and we do not promise analytics your hardware cannot deliver.

Can I view my cameras on my phone?

Yes — remote mobile viewing is configured as part of installation where your system supports it, and we show you how to use live viewing and playback.

What is the difference between DVR and NVR?

A DVR works with analogue cameras; an NVR works with IP network cameras. The right choice depends on your camera system.

How long is installation?

Most home and small-shop installations are completed in a day; larger commercial systems take longer. The timeline is confirmed in your proposal.

Do you repair existing CCTV systems?

Yes — we diagnose faults in cameras, recorders, cabling and power supplies, and repair or replace only what is needed.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes — regular checks, cleaning, storage review and fault prevention are available for existing systems.

Which areas of Lahore do you serve?

We serve DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Wapda Town and Bahria Town Lahore, and other Lahore areas on confirmation.
