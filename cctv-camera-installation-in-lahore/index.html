CCTV Camera Installation in Lahore

SmartShield Security installs CCTV camera systems for homes, offices, shops and commercial properties across Lahore. This page brings everything together: what we install, how the process works, what it typically costs, and which areas we serve.

What We Install

Complete CCTV systems — dome, bullet, PTZ, IP and Wi-Fi cameras, DVR and NVR recorders, hard-disk storage, power supplies and mobile viewing. We also install alarm systems, fire alarm systems and biometric attendance machines for offices that need them.

How Installation Works

  1. Share your requirements — property type, entrances and what you want covered.
  2. Assessment — we recommend camera positions and equipment.
  3. Package and budget proposal — a preliminary estimate, then a final quotation after site assessment.
  4. Installation and testing — clean cabling, recorder setup, mobile viewing configured on your phone, and every camera tested before handover.

What It Typically Costs

Professional installation for basic setups typically starts from PKR 12,500 in Lahore. Most home and small-shop installations fall between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000 or more, depending on camera count, DVR/NVR system and mobile setup. A 4MP camera typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, with installation separate. These are typical market ranges for guidance — your final price is confirmed after a site assessment.

Common Problems We Avoid

Poor video quality, unstable connections, bad camera angles, power outages, mobile apps that will not connect and recording failures — most of these come from poor installation or wrong equipment, not from the camera itself. Our process is designed to prevent them: correct camera choice, proper placement, tested connections and storage sized to your retention period.
