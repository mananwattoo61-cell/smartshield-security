CCTV Maintenance and Repair in Lahore

CCTV systems fail quietly — a lens gets dusty, a hard disk fills up, a camera drifts out of position. SmartShield Security maintains and repairs CCTV systems across Lahore, including systems installed by other companies.

CCTV Repair

When a camera goes dark, a recorder stops saving footage or remote viewing stops working, the fault is usually in one place — a camera, a cable, a power supply or a setting. We diagnose the fault first, explain what is wrong, and repair or replace only what is needed.

CCTV repair service

CCTV Maintenance

Regular maintenance catches problems before footage is needed: camera cleaning and re-alignment, recorder and hard-disk checks, cable and power-supply inspection, and a review of settings and recording. You receive a short report after each visit.

CCTV maintenance service

Common Faults We Fix

  • Cameras offline or showing no picture
  • Recorder not saving or playing back footage
  • Mobile app not connecting
  • Blurry or poor-quality night video
  • Power supply and cabling faults
  • Hard disk full or faulty

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV repair cost in Lahore?

It depends on the fault — a cable or power supply is usually a small repair, while a recorder or hard-disk replacement costs more. We diagnose first and explain the fault and the fix before any work starts.

Do you repair systems installed by other companies?

Yes. We maintain and repair CCTV systems regardless of who installed them, provided compatible parts are available.

How often should CCTV be maintained?

For most homes and shops, a check every few months is enough. Busy commercial sites and outdoor cameras benefit from more frequent checks.
