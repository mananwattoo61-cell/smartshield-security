2MP CCTV Camera Price in Lahore

A 2MP camera records in Full HD (1080p) and is the most common choice for homes and small shops in Lahore. It gives clear video for entrances, rooms and counters at a lower cost than higher-resolution cameras — and it needs less storage than 4MP or 4K systems.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 2MP camera good enough for home security?

For most homes, yes. 2MP Full HD video is clear enough for entrances, driveways and rooms. Higher resolution mainly helps when you need fine detail at a distance.

How much does a 2MP CCTV camera cost in Lahore?

Prices vary by brand, housing and features. As a reference, a 4MP camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, and 2MP cameras generally cost less than 4MP models. Installation is charged separately.

Does a 2MP camera work at night?

Most 2MP cameras include infrared night vision. Check the stated night vision range and whether the camera is placed where its infrared light can reach.
