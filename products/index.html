CCTV Products in Lahore

SmartShield Security supplies and installs CCTV products chosen for your property — not a fixed kit. Below is an overview of the products we work with. Brands commonly recommended in Pakistan include Hikvision, Dahua, IMOU and CP Plus.

Cameras

Dome Cameras

Discreet, wide-angle coverage for indoors — shops, offices, hallways and living areas.

Bullet Cameras

Long-range outdoor coverage for boundaries, driveways, gates and entrances.

PTZ Cameras

Pan, tilt and zoom for large open areas that need active, adjustable coverage.

IP Cameras

High-resolution network cameras with Power over Ethernet and remote viewing.

AI-Enabled Cameras

Human and vehicle detection and smart alerts — only where camera and recorder both support them.

Wi-Fi Cameras

Easy setup with no heavy wiring and direct mobile app connectivity — best where Wi-Fi signal is strong.

Recorders & Storage

  • DVR — for analogue / HD camera systems
  • NVR — for IP network camera systems
  • Hard disks — sized to your camera count and retention period (7, 15, 30 or 60 days)

Frequently Asked Questions

Which CCTV camera brand is best in Pakistan?

Hikvision, Dahua, IMOU and CP Plus are among the best-regarded brands in Pakistan for reliability and clear footage. The right brand for you depends on budget, camera type and the features you need.

Do you supply the cameras or only install them?

We supply and install as a complete package, and we also install and configure systems where equipment is already available. The package proposal lists the equipment included.

Can I view my cameras on my phone?

Yes, where your system supports remote viewing, we configure mobile access and show you how to use live viewing and playback.
