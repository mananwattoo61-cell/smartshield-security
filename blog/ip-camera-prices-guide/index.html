IP Camera Prices Guide for Lahore

Published 11 November 2026 · SmartShield Security, Lahore

IP camera prices in Lahore: what IP cameras cost, what affects the price, PoE and NVR requirements, and when IP is worth it for your property.

What Is an IP Camera?

An IP camera sends video over your network instead of a direct cable to a recorder. That gives higher-resolution images, flexible placement and remote viewing — and it makes the system easy to grow, because adding a camera is mostly a network job. IP cameras are the standard choice for offices, larger homes and commercial properties in Lahore.

What Do IP Cameras Cost in Lahore?

IP cameras cost more than analogue cameras of the same resolution, because they contain their own network hardware. Prices vary by resolution (2MP, 4MP, 5MP or 4K), brand and features. As a reference, a 4MP camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, and IP models are usually priced above analogue equivalents. Installation for a complete system in Lahore usually ranges between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000 or more, depending on camera count, recorder and mobile setup.

What Else Does an IP System Need?

  • NVR — a network video recorder with enough channels for your cameras.
  • PoE switch — sends power and video through one network cable per camera.
  • Cat6 cabling — proper network cable, not telephone wire.
  • Hard disk — sized to camera count, resolution and retention period.
  • Network planning — so cameras stay connected and do not drop offline.

When Is IP Worth It?

IP is worth it when you need sharp video, many cameras, or a system that will grow. It is also the right choice when cameras are far apart or in different buildings on the same network. For a simple 2-camera home setup, an analogue or wireless system may do the same job for less money.

Common IP Mistakes

The most common IP problems are weak networks, cheap switches and recorder incompatibility. A camera that keeps disconnecting is usually a network problem, not a camera problem. Proper planning — PoE capacity, cable quality and NVR compatibility — is part of every SmartShield IP installation, and we test every camera before handover.

IP Cameras and Your Internet Connection

Recording happens on your local network, so a slow internet connection does not affect recording quality. Internet speed only matters when you watch cameras remotely — and even then, the app usually streams a lower-resolution preview to save data. What matters more is the quality of your home or office network: a good router and proper cabling keep the system stable.
