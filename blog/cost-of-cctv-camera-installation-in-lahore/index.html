Cost of CCTV Camera Installation in Lahore

Published 11 November 2026 · SmartShield Security, Lahore

How much does CCTV camera installation cost in Lahore? Typical prices, what affects the cost, and how to get an accurate quotation.

What Does CCTV Installation Cost in Lahore?

There is no single fixed price for CCTV installation, because every property is different. As a guide, professional installation for basic setups in Lahore typically starts from PKR 12,500, and most home and small-shop installations fall between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000 or more. The final price depends on how many cameras you need, what type they are, which recorder and storage you choose, and how much cabling the job requires.

What Affects the Cost?

  • Number of cameras — a 2-camera home setup costs far less than an 8-camera office system.
  • Camera type and resolution — 2MP cameras cost less than 4MP, 5MP or 4K models. A 4MP camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, with installation separate.
  • Recorder and storage — a DVR or NVR plus a hard disk sized to your retention period (7, 15, 30 or 60 days).
  • Cabling and installation complexity — long cable runs, multiple floors and outdoor work add labour and materials.
  • Mobile viewing setup — configuring remote viewing on your phone is part of a professional installation.

Home vs Shop vs Office

A small home usually needs 2–4 cameras covering the gate, driveway and main entrance. A shop adds the cash counter and stock room. An office adds reception, corridors and server areas. Each extra camera adds the camera itself, cabling and a recorder channel — so the clearest way to control cost is to cover the areas that matter and skip the ones that do not.

Hidden Costs to Watch For

Cheap quotations sometimes leave out the hard disk, the power supply or mobile setup, and those appear as extras later. Always ask what is included: cameras, recorder, hard disk, cabling, power supply, installation and mobile viewing. A proper quotation lists every item.
