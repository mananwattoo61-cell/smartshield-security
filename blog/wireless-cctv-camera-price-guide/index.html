Wireless CCTV Camera Price Guide for Lahore

Published 11 November 2026 · SmartShield Security, Lahore

Wireless CCTV camera prices in Lahore: what wireless cameras cost, what affects the price, and when wireless is the right choice for your home.

What Is a Wireless CCTV Camera?

A wireless camera connects to your Wi-Fi network instead of a long cable run. It still needs power — from a nearby socket or a battery — but the video travels wirelessly to your recorder, memory card or the viewing app. Installation is faster and cleaner, with no drilling for cables through walls.

What Do Wireless Cameras Cost in Lahore?

Wireless cameras are often priced similarly to wired cameras of the same resolution; the saving is usually in installation, not in the camera itself. A basic home wireless setup in Lahore typically starts from PKR 12,500 for installation, with the total depending on camera count, resolution and storage. As a reference, a 4MP camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, with installation separate.

What Affects the Price?

  • Resolution — 2MP is the most affordable; 4MP costs more.
  • Indoor or outdoor — weatherproof outdoor models cost more.
  • Power — battery-powered models cost more than mains-powered ones.
  • Storage — memory card, cloud subscription or NVR recording.
  • Wi-Fi coverage — weak signal areas may need a range extender or access point.

When Wireless Is the Right Choice

Wireless works best in homes, rented properties and small shops where running cables is difficult or not allowed, and where the Wi-Fi signal is strong at every camera position. It is also a good choice for a first camera or a small 2–4 camera system.

When Wireless Is Not Ideal

Thick walls, long distances and interference can make cameras disconnect — the most common wireless fault we are called to fix. Large properties, many cameras and outdoor boundaries usually suit wired or IP systems better. We test signal strength at each camera position before recommending wireless, so you do not discover the problem after installation.

Battery vs Mains Power

Battery-powered wireless cameras are the easiest to place — no socket needed — but the battery must be recharged every few weeks or months depending on use, and cold weather shortens battery life. Mains-powered cameras never need charging, but they need a socket nearby. For a camera that must record all the time, mains power is usually the better choice; battery models suit places where a socket is impossible.
