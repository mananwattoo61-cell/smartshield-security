CCTV Installation for Shops in Lahore

Published 11 November 2026 · SmartShield Security, Lahore

CCTV installation for shops in Lahore: cash counter, entrance and stock room coverage, costs, and what to check before buying.

What a Shop Needs Covered

For a shop, three positions matter most: the cash counter, the entrance and the stock room. The cash counter camera should show the till and the customer clearly. The entrance camera should show who comes in and out. The stock room camera protects inventory. A fourth camera on the sales floor covers the rest. Most small shops are well covered with 4 cameras.

Choosing Cameras for a Shop

Dome cameras suit the shop floor and counter — wide, discreet coverage. A bullet camera suits the entrance and any outdoor area. Night vision matters if the shop opens early or closes late. Resolution should be high enough to identify a person at the counter: 2MP is usually enough at short range, while 4MP gives more detail.

What Does Shop CCTV Cost?

Most small-shop installations in Lahore fall between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000, depending on camera count, recorder and storage. A 4MP camera typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000, with installation separate. The final price is confirmed after a site assessment — use the Smart Calculator for a preliminary estimate.

Recording and Playback

Choose a retention period that fits how your shop works — 15–30 days is common, so you can review an incident discovered days later. Make sure the hard disk is large enough for your camera count and retention period, and test playback before the installer leaves. A system that records but cannot play back properly is useless when you need it.

After Installation

Keep lenses clean, check that recording is working every week or two, and keep the recorder in a safe, ventilated place — not on the shop floor where it can be damaged or unplugged. We offer maintenance visits that cover cleaning, storage checks and fault prevention.

Common Shop Mistakes to Avoid

The most common mistake is pointing the counter camera at the customer's back instead of the till — the footage then shows nothing useful when a dispute arises. Another is buying the cheapest hard disk and discovering a week later that it only keeps two days of recordings. And a third is never testing playback: a system can look fine on the live screen while recording nothing at all. Test playback on installation day, and check it again after the first week.
