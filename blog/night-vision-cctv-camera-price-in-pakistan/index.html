Night Vision CCTV Camera Price in Pakistan

Published 11 November 2026 · SmartShield Security, Lahore

Night vision CCTV camera prices in Pakistan: infrared vs colour night vision, what affects the price, and how to choose for your home or shop.

Why Night Vision Matters

Most security incidents happen after dark, so a camera that cannot see at night is only half a security system. Night vision cameras use infrared light or a white-light LED to record in darkness, and the quality of that night picture is often more important than the daytime picture.

Infrared vs Colour Night Vision

Infrared night vision records in black and white using invisible infrared light. It works in complete darkness and is the most common option. Colour night vision uses a visible white light or a sensitive sensor to record in colour at night — useful when you need to identify clothing colours or vehicle colours, but it needs some ambient light or its own LED, and it can disturb neighbours if the light is bright.

What Do Night Vision Cameras Cost?

Night vision is now standard on most cameras, so it does not add a large premium by itself. Prices depend mainly on resolution, brand and night vision range. As a reference, a 4MP camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000 depending on brand and features, with installation separate. Colour night vision models usually cost more than basic infrared models.

What Affects Night Picture Quality?

  • Infrared range — stated in metres; the camera must be close enough for its own light to reach the subject.
  • Placement — infrared reflects off glass and walls, so cameras should not face windows directly.
  • Lighting — a small amount of ambient light improves the picture noticeably.
  • Lens and sensor quality — a better sensor gives a cleaner night image with less noise.

Choosing a Night Vision Camera

For a gate or driveway, choose an infrared camera with a range that covers the full distance. For a shop entrance or a well-lit street, colour night vision can be worth the extra cost. Tell us where the camera will sit and how dark the area gets at night, and we will recommend a model that actually sees what you need it to see.

Testing Night Vision Before You Buy

Night vision specifications are measured in ideal conditions, so the real picture can differ. If possible, ask to see a sample recording from the same model at night, or have the installer demonstrate the camera after dark during installation. A camera that looks good in the shop may not perform the same way in your unlit driveway.
