How to Choose a Package

  • 2MP — best value for homes and small shops; clear Full HD video.
  • 5MP — more detail for entrances, parking and shop floors.
  • 4K / 8MP — finest detail for large properties and high-security entrances.
  • IP — highest flexibility and resolution, for properties that need a network system.
  • Wireless — fastest installation where cabling is difficult, provided Wi-Fi is strong.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a CCTV package cost in Lahore?

Installation typically starts from PKR 12,500, and most home and small-shop installations fall between PKR 12,500 and PKR 50,000+. The exact price depends on camera count, resolution, recorder, storage and cable length, and is confirmed after a site survey.

What is included in a CCTV package?

Cameras, a compatible DVR or NVR recorder, a hard disk sized to your retention period, cabling, professional installation and mobile viewing setup. The exact list is in your package proposal.

Which package is best for a home?

For most homes, a 2MP or 4MP package with 4 cameras covers the main gate, driveway and entrances. Larger houses usually need 6 or 8 cameras. The calculator can help you decide.

Can I add more cameras later?

Yes — IP systems are the easiest to expand, and most DVR/NVR recorders have spare channels. Tell us your future plans when choosing a recorder.
