About SmartShield Security

SmartShield Security provides CCTV camera installation and complete security solutions in Lahore, Pakistan.

We work with homes, offices, shops, schools, warehouses and commercial properties. Our focus is straightforward: the right cameras in the right places, installed neatly, configured properly, and supported after handover.

What We Do

  • CCTV camera installation for homes, offices and shops
  • IP, HD and AI-enabled camera systems, where compatible
  • DVR and NVR installation and configuration
  • Remote mobile viewing setup
  • CCTV repair and maintenance

How We Work

We start by understanding your property and what needs to be covered, recommend a camera layout and package, install and test the system, and show you how to use viewing and playback.

We do not publish invented experience claims, customer numbers or credentials. What we promise is careful planning, professional installation and honest advice about what your equipment can do.
