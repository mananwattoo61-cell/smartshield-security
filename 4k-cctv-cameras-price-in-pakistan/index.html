4K CCTV Cameras Price in Pakistan

A 4K (8MP) camera records roughly four times the detail of a 2MP camera. That matters when you need to identify faces or read number plates from a distance — for example at a main gate, a parking area or a large commercial boundary. For a small room or a shop counter, 4K is often more than you need.

What Affects the Price?

  • Camera brand, sensor and lens quality
  • Recorder must support 4K — a 2MP DVR will not record 4K
  • Hard-disk storage: 4K footage needs much more space
  • Network and cabling quality for IP 4K systems
  • Number of cameras and installation complexity

Typical Price Ranges in Lahore

4K camera (typical)

4K cameras cost more than 2MP and 4MP models; the exact price depends on brand, sensor and features.

4MP comparison

A 4MP CCTV camera in Pakistan typically costs PKR 6,000–12,000; 4K cameras sit above this range.

Full 4K system

A complete 4K system costs more overall because the recorder and hard disk must also be 4K-capable.

These are typical market ranges for guidance, not SmartShield fixed prices. Final pricing is confirmed after a site assessment.

4K is worth it for large areas, long distances and high-security entrances. For most homes and small shops, a good 2MP or 4MP system gives clear, usable video at a lower total cost. Read night vision camera prices and our DVR vs NVR guide before deciding.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 4K CCTV camera worth it?

It is worth it when you need fine detail at a distance — faces, number plates, large open areas. For small rooms and short distances, 2MP or 4MP is usually enough.

Do I need a special DVR for 4K cameras?

Yes. The recorder must support 4K resolution, and the hard disk must be large enough for 4K footage at your chosen retention period.

Does 4K use more internet for remote viewing?

Remote viewing uses bandwidth when you watch live or play back. 4K streams use more data than 2MP streams, so a stable connection matters.
