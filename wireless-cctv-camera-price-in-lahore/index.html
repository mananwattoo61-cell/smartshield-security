FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Wi-Fi CCTV camera good?

Yes. Wi-Fi CCTV cameras are convenient for homes and small offices — easy setup, no heavy wiring and direct mobile app connectivity for remote monitoring, provided the Wi-Fi signal is strong.

Do wireless cameras work during load shedding?

Battery-powered models keep working for a while; mains-powered cameras stop unless there is backup power. Recording also depends on the recorder or storage having power.

Can a wireless camera record without Wi-Fi?

Most wireless cameras need Wi-Fi to send video. Some record to a memory card in the camera; check the model before buying.
